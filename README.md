# Far From Home — Five-chapter interactive documentary

ENGL 1301 | Texas Tech University | October 2026

## How to view and publish
Open `index.html` or publish the entire folder through GitHub Pages (main branch, repository root). Keep the `assets` directory and all `.css` and `.js` files together. Each chapter audio player plays a separate short MP3 clip from `assets/moments/`. Keep these clips alongside the four full interview recordings used in the source archive.

## Story structure
1. Arriving in Lubbock — expectations and actual first impressions
2. Finding Your People — library, TOR, work, friendships and TTU services
3. Life Beyond Campus — mobility, transport, cultural and financial access
4. What Makes a Place Home? — equal-weight interactive comparison of four voices
5. What I Discovered — interviewer's research interpretation and questions still open
Then: a supplementary illustrated reflection, “Three spaces. More questions.” It compares interview evidence with three archival photographs and identifies unanswered questions, without claiming firsthand site observations. The source archive follows with four complete recordings, transcripts, methods, references and photograph credits.

## Files
- `index.html`: documentary narrative, accessible chapter headings, photos, transcripts and sources.
- `styles.css`: original editorial design.
- `chapters.css`: layout for new chapters, contextual listening, and four voices.
- `script.js`: mobile navigation.
- `chapters.js`: interactive four-voice comparison.
- `audio-player.css` and `audio-player.js`: shared audio controls for the five chapter clips and four full interviews, with seeking, mute, playback speed and native controls when JavaScript is unavailable.
- `field-notes.css`: responsive supplementary research reflection and three archival photo cards.
- `assets/`: all four source recordings, including `interview-04.m4a` (public-sharing consent confirmed by interviewer).
- `assets/moments/`: five short MP3 excerpts used by the chapter audio players.
- `assets/field-notes/`: locally stored, credited campus reference photographs; see the attribution README in that folder.
- `Interview_Transcripts_Accessibility.docx`: class accessibility transcript (review caption edits before submission).
- `Statement_of_Goals_and_Choices.docx`: prior editable statement; review/update it to describe this five-chapter version.

## Academic and ethical notes
- Four student interviews are primary sources, not a representative sample of international students at TTU.
- Interviews 03 and 04 have portions edited/paraphrased from automatic captions and are not word-for-word transcripts.
- Campus photos are credited licensed archival references, not firsthand field observations.
- The interviewer has confirmed permission to use the TOR photographs in this project. Keep source credit visible and retain the permission confirmation with the project records.
- Some chapter clips include brief question/prompt context; review them against the original recordings in a final listen.
